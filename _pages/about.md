I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence with a deep passion for applying machine learning to solve critical challenges in modern medicine. My goal is to leverage computational models to create diagnostic tools that are more accessible, accurate, and capable of improving patient outcomes.

My interest in this field is rooted in a long-held ambition to contribute to human health. From an early age, I was driven by a desire to help alleviate suffering from disease. The discovery of artificial intelligence marked a turning point for me, revealing its profound potential to fulfill this ambition at a global scale. I am deeply motivated by the synergy between medicine's compassionate goals and machine learning's analytical power, and I am committed to being at the forefront of this transformative field.

To pursue this mission, I have cultivated a strong technical foundation centered around the Python ecosystem:

* **Core Machine Learning:** I am proficient in Python and its essential libraries for data analysis and model development, including **TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Pandas,** and **NumPy**.
* **Medical Imaging:** I have focused experience in computer vision for healthcare, including working with medical data formats like **DICOM** and utilizing specialized libraries such as **MONAI** and **OpenCV** for image segmentation and classification tasks.
* **Software & Deployment:** I believe in building tangible tools. I have experience in developing proof-of-concept web applications with **Flask** and containerizing them with **Docker** to ensure reproducibility and ease of deployment.

I am currently seeking a Ph.D. position where I can apply these skills to cutting-edge research. I am particularly drawn to the challenges of explainable AI (XAI) in clinical settings and the use of generative models to address data scarcity in medical imaging.
